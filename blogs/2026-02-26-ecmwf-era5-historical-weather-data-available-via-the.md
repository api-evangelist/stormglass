Stormglass has added ECMWF ERA5 to its historical weather data offering, providing research-grade, high-resolution data from 2014 onward at 0.25 degree spatial resolution. ERA5, developed by the European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts, is accessible through the API's Historical Endpoint and reconstructs meteorological parameters from satellite observations, weather stations, and ship data.
